THE BODY OF WORK

An overview of the work itself

Not a Pulse overview — an account of what was produced, in what order, and why this body of work exists.

Pulse is a cash flow operating system for Canadian small businesses and startup founders — a system that watches the financial pulse of a business continuously, detects what is wrong before it becomes a crisis, and tells the owner in plain language, on their phone, without requiring them to log in. Everything below was specified before any production code: the problem with proof, market sizing with current Canadian data, a complete database schema, a six-phase nightly pipeline, fifteen metric formulas each derived from first principles, a four-client worked example proving the arithmetic, an integration reference, and an eighteen-competitor landscape.
